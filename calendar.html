Setup

Settings are saved only in this browser (localStorage), never in the public repo, so your address isn't published. Google decides what the embed shows. If you're signed in to Google in this browser with access to the calendar, you see your events. Anyone else sees only what you've made public (or an error if nothing is public). Events update as Google updates the embed, and this page reloads it every 5 minutes.
