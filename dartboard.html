Three paper portfolios started with the same fake $10,000 on the same day: the owner's hand-picked watchlist, eleven S&P 500 stocks chosen by a seeded random dart (the seed is recorded — anyone can reproduce the throw), and simply buying SPY. The classic index-fund question, run on real data: does picking beat not picking? Alpha here means realized return minus SPY's — what actually happened, never a projection. Paper money, educational display, not investment advice.
