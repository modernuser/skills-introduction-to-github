How to tell signal from noise (checklist)
  • Primary source? A company filing, press release, or earnings report beats any retelling of one.
  • Named sources? "The company said" is checkable; "sources say" and "analysts believe" often aren't.
  • Falsifiable claim? "$250B investment through 2035" can be verified. "Set to soar" cannot.
  • Price-target clickbait? Headlines with predictions in them are marketing, not reporting.
  • Cross-check: if a big claim appears in only one outlet, wait for a second independent one.
